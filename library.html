What lands first

We would rather open with a small, finished shelf than a large, half-written one. The first release is deliberately narrow.

  1. All fourteen past papers, with mark schemes and marker notes
  2. Teardowns for Helio, Gust and Store, including full schematics
  3. The first hundred build logs, sorted by kit rather than by date
  4. Reading paths for the cell, integrals and static equilibrium
  5. Risk assessments for every product in the energy line

Everything else follows as it gets written. Nothing goes behind a login.

Waiting list
