01What it is

You show up in the morning with nothing and leave with a project you made.

A hackathon is a day where you team up, pick a problem and build something for it: a website, an app, a game, a robot, a tool you actually wish existed. It doesn't have to be finished or polished. It has to be yours.

You don't need to know how to code already. If you've never written a line, come anyway. There'll be people around to help you get started, and plenty of first-timers in the room.

NextGen STEM is run by high school students. We put this together because we wanted one near us.

04Questions

Who can come?

Any high school student in grades 9 through 12. You don't need to be from a particular school.

Does it cost anything?

No. Registering and attending are both free.

I've never coded. Is this for me?

Yes. Lots of people at hackathons are beginners. Pick something small, ask questions and learn as you go. That's the point of the day.

Do I need a team?

No. Teams are up to four people. If you don't have one, tell us on the form and we'll help you find one at kickoff. You can also work alone.

Can I start my project before the day?

You can think about ideas all you want, but the building itself has to happen during the event. That keeps it fair for everyone.

What are the rules?

Be kind, build your own work, and follow the Hack Club Code of Conduct. Anyone who doesn't will be asked to leave.

My parents have questions.

Have them email us at nextgenstem42@gmail.com. We're happy to talk.
