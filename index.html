The argument

Instructions tell you someone already knows the answer.

A kit with a manual has one right answer. We would rather give you parts, one rule, and get out of the way.

Four rules we build everything around.

01

Ship the constraint, not the solution

Every kit gives you a limit. How you work inside it is up to you.

02

Make it measurable, or don't ship it

If you cannot put a number on it, you cannot tell if it got better.

03

Depth beats coverage, every time

Forty topics skimmed teaches recall. One topic pushed hard teaches thinking.

04

Failure is a data point, not a defect

Parts are cheap on purpose. A bridge that breaks at 4.2 kg tells you more than one you never tested.
