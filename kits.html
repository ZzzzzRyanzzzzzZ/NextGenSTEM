Inside

What's in every box

Four things, and one of them is deliberately blank. The absence of a manual isn't a cost saving. The log book costs us more to print than instructions would.

  1. The parts

    Sorted, counted, and listed with tolerances, so a build that doesn't work can be blamed on the right thing.

  2. A constraint card

    One side states the limit. The other lists five things people have built. Neither side explains how.

  3. A blank log book

    Forty pages, ruled and dated, for the measurements. Most of it will record things that didn't work.

  4. A part number sheet

    Every component sourced and named, so a broken piece is a $2 order rather than a dead kit.
