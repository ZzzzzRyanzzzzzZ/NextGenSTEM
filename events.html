What to expect

Four kinds
of event.

Everything we run follows the same rule as everything we send: no instructions, something to measure, and a reason to care. These are the formats we are planning, in roughly the order we expect to run them.

01
Build days
In person

A room, a kit per pair, and a whole day. The same constraint for everyone, so by four o'clock there are twelve different answers on the tables and an argument about which one is best.

02
Competition sittings
In person · Online

The open round of each single-topic competition, sat together. Ninety minutes, one subject, and the full solutions talked through afterwards while everyone still cares about the answer.

03
Teardown evenings
Online

We take apart something real — a drill, a solar charger, a bike computer — on camera, name every part, and work out from the inside why it was designed the way it was.

04
Fellowship showcase
In person · Online

The end of each cohort. Every fellow shows what they built, what the numbers were, and what failed on the way. The best builds go into the Library with credit.

Run one with us

Have a room
and a Saturday?

A school, a library, a maker space or a garage with a big enough table. If you can host, we can bring kits, a brief and someone to run it. Write to us with where you are and how many people the room takes — use the application form and say so in the last question — and we will work out a date.
