The format

Three rounds,
each one deeper

Round one rewards a student who paid attention. Round two rewards one who can explain. Round three has no published answer, and the best submissions get added to the Library with the author's name on them.

  1. Open

    90 minutes, 20 questions, sat online or on paper at school. No calculators for the maths rounds, no notes for any of them. Roughly a third of entrants go through.

    Length
    90 min
    Marked by
    Machine

  2. Depth

    Four extended problems over a week, written up properly. The reasoning carries the marks. A correct answer with no argument scores about a third of one that is wrong but well-made.

    Length
    7 days
    Marked by
    Two humans

  3. Open question

    One problem with no known clean solution, tackled in teams of up to three over a month. Judged on the quality of the attack, not on whether it landed.

    Length
    4 weeks
    Marked by
    Panel of 5

What it looks like

Three real
questions

One from each round, taken from papers we have already run. The jump between them is the whole idea: same subtopic, three different demands.

Round 1 · Open
Osmosis

A plant cell is placed in a solution. Its volume falls by 12% over 90 seconds and then holds steady. Give two mechanisms consistent with that curve, and name one measurement that would tell them apart.

Primes

Rules & eligibility

Free to enter, and it stays free.

Open to anyone in grades 7 to 12, anywhere, school or not. No entry fee, no minimum team size, no requirement to buy anything we sell. The papers have nothing to do with our hardware.

Sit round one online in your own time on the day, or on paper if a teacher is invigilating. Rounds two and three are written up at home. Anything you may use is listed on the front page of each paper; everything else is out.

For teachers

Host a round

Running it in a classroom takes one room, one invigilator, and about two hours including settling. We send the papers, the answer sheets and the mark scheme; you send back scans or nothing at all, if your students sit it online.

  1. Register the school, roughly five weeks ahead
  2. Papers arrive sealed, ten days before the date
  3. Sit it in one 90-minute block, any time that day
  4. Results and full solutions come back inside a fortnight
