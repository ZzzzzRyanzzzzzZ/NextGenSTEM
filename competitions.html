Rules & eligibility

Free to enter, always.

Open to anyone in grades 7 to 12, with or without a school. No fee, and nothing to buy.

Turn up and we hand you the kit and the brief. What you may use is listed on it.

For teachers

Host a round

You need one room, one adult and about two hours. We bring everything else.

  1. Register the school, roughly five weeks ahead
  2. We bring the kits and the brief, one box per entrant
  3. Run it as one 90-minute block, any time that day
  4. Results and full solutions come back inside a fortnight
