How it works

Three steps,
one of them yours.

A fellowship is not a giveaway. We are buying the thing you make with hardware and attention, and the only currency we want back is a finished build with its working shown.

  1. Apply

    Pick a field, answer three short questions. Twenty minutes. No essay, no reference letter.

  2. Get selected

    We read everything and email a yes or a no. A no comes with a reason, because a form letter helps nobody.

  3. Build

    The kit arrives with your field's brief. Six weeks. Outside parts allowed, and often expected.

The six fields

Every field runs
its own competition.

You apply to one field. The kit you receive and the brief you answer both follow from that choice, and at the end you are ranked only against the others in your field, never against a bridge builder if what you built was a spectrometer.

Judging is blind on names and schools. The brief's stated measurement is 60% of the mark; the reasoning you show is the other 40%.

01
Mechanisms
Open

Kit: Linkage. Brief: move a 500 g load one metre across a table on stored energy alone. No motors, no batteries.

Measured on: distance travelled per joule stored.

02
Circuits & sensing
Open

Kit: Sense. Brief: build an instrument that measures something you cannot see, and show that it agrees with a known reference.

Measured on: agreement with the reference, across the range.

03
Renewable energy
Open

Kit: Helio or Gust. Brief: harvest power by day, store it, and run a load through the night. Report a real round-trip efficiency.

Measured on: watt-hours delivered per watt-hour captured.

04
Structures
Open

Kit: Span. Brief: cross a 400 mm gap carrying as much as you can. Then load it until it breaks, and film the failure.

Measured on: grams carried per gram of structure.

05
Optics
Open

Kit: Ray. Brief: form a readable image of a distant object, then tell us precisely what is limiting its sharpness.

Measured on: resolved line pairs, and the honesty of the limit analysis.

06
Growth systems
Open

Kit: Cycle. Brief: keep a plant alive for six weeks inside a closed loop, logging every input you give it.

Measured on: biomass gained per litre of water spent.

Cohort 01 · 2026–27

Twelve weeks from the form to the finished thing.

We run one cohort at a time, so that every application gets read properly rather than skimmed. If you miss this one, the form stays open and rolls into the next.

  1. Rolling

    Applications open

    Submit whenever you are ready. They are read in batches every fortnight rather than left to pile up.

  2. +3 weeks

    Decisions by email

    Yes or no, written by a person, with the reason attached. A no does not stop you applying to the next cohort.

  3. +4 weeks

    Kits ship

    Your field's kit, its brief, and a blank logbook. Postage is on us and there is nothing to pay on delivery.

  4. +10 weeks

    Builds due

    Photographs, your numbers, and one page on what you tried that did not work. Six weeks of build time, and we do not chase you.

  5. +12 weeks

    Field results

    Judged inside your field. Every entrant gets written feedback, and the strongest builds go into the Library with credit.

Who it is for

Fewer rules
than you expect.

If you are unsure whether you qualify, apply anyway. The worst outcome is a reply that says not yet.

  1. Ages 11 to 18. Any school, homeschool included, anywhere we can post a box to.
  2. No grades, no transcript, no reference letter. We will never ask you for one.
  3. Apply alone or as a pair. A pair shares one kit, one brief and one entry.
  4. Prior experience is genuinely not required. Say so honestly and it counts against nothing.
  5. Under 16 needs a parent or guardian to sign off before a kit can ship.
  6. You keep the kit, whatever you build with it, and everything you learned on the way.

After you send it

What the reply
looks like.

If it is a yes

An email confirming your field, the brief in full, the date your kit ships and the date your build is due. Reply with a postal address and that is the last piece of admin there is.

If it is not yet

An email that says which part of the application was thin, and what would make the next one stronger. Reapplying to the next cohort is normal and counts in your favour.

Either way it arrives within three weeks. If a month passes with nothing, write to diwanryan13@gmail.com. Something went wrong on our end, not yours.
