How it works

Three steps,
one of them yours.

It is not a giveaway. We pay in hardware and attention, and what we want back is a finished build.

  1. Apply

    Say which competition you are entering and answer three questions. Twenty minutes. No essay, no reference letter.

  2. Get selected

    We read everything and email a yes or a no, with a reason either way.

  3. Build

    The kit arrives with your field's brief. Six weeks. Outside parts allowed, and often expected.

The six fields

Every field runs
its own competition.

You pick one field. That decides your kit and your brief, and you are only judged against others in the same field.

Names and schools are hidden. The measurement is 60% of the mark, your reasoning the other 40%.

01
Mechanisms
Open

Kit: Linkage. Brief: move a 500 g load one metre across a table on stored energy alone. No motors, no batteries.

Measured on: distance travelled per joule stored.

02
Circuits & sensing
Open

Kit: Sense. Brief: build an instrument that measures something you cannot see, and show that it agrees with a known reference.

Measured on: agreement with the reference, across the range.

03
Renewable energy
Open

Kit: Helio or Gust. Brief: harvest power by day, store it, and run a load through the night. Report a real round-trip efficiency.

Measured on: watt-hours delivered per watt-hour captured.

04
Structures
Open

Kit: Span. Brief: cross a 400 mm gap carrying as much as you can. Then load it until it breaks, and film the failure.

Measured on: grams carried per gram of structure.

05
Optics
Open

Kit: Ray. Brief: form a readable image of a distant object, then tell us precisely what is limiting its sharpness.

Measured on: resolved line pairs, and the honesty of the limit analysis.

06
Growth systems
Open

Kit: Cycle. Brief: keep a plant alive for six weeks inside a closed loop, logging every input you give it.

Measured on: biomass gained per litre of water spent.

Cohort 01 · 2026–27

Twelve weeks from the form to the finished thing.

One group at a time, so every application gets read properly. Miss this one and you roll into the next.

  1. Rolling

    Applications open

    Send it whenever you are ready. We read them every two weeks.

  2. +3 weeks

    Decisions by email

    A person replies either way, with a reason. A no does not stop you applying again.

  3. +4 weeks

    Kits ship

    Your kit, your brief and a blank logbook. Postage is on us.

  4. +10 weeks

    Builds due

    Photos, your numbers, and a page on what did not work. Six weeks, and we will not chase you.

  5. +12 weeks

    Field results

    Everyone gets written feedback. The best builds get published with credit.

Who it is for

Fewer rules
than you expect.

Not sure if you qualify? Apply anyway. The worst answer is not yet.

  1. Ages 11 to 18. Any school, homeschool included, anywhere we can post a box to.
  2. No grades, no transcript, no reference letter. We will never ask you for one.
  3. Apply alone or as a pair. A pair shares one kit, one brief and one entry.
  4. Prior experience is genuinely not required. Say so honestly and it counts against nothing.
  5. Under 16 needs a parent or guardian to sign off before a kit can ship.
  6. You keep the kit, whatever you build with it, and everything you learned on the way.
  7. Not selected for a free kit? You can still enter your field's competition with your own parts. The application is for the kit, not the door.
The application

Twenty minutes,
honestly answered.

Write it like you would explain it to a friend. We are not marking your grammar.

It asks who you are, which competition you want, and three short questions. One inbox, one person reading. We do not share it or add you to a mailing list.

After you send it

What the reply
looks like.

If it is a yes

An email confirming your field, your brief, when the kit ships and when the build is due.

If it is not yet

An email saying which part was thin and what would make the next one stronger. Reapplying counts in your favour.

Either way you hear within three weeks. Nothing after a month? Write to nextgenstem42@gmail.com.
